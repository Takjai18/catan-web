========================================
  Settlers of Catan — USB Portable
========================================

QUICK START (Windows)
1. Open folder: Catan_Katan_Island
2. Double-click: Play_Catan.bat
   Or double-click: index.html

If the page is blank:
- Run Play_Catan_Server.bat instead
- Browser opens http://localhost:8765/

Mac: double-click index.html

FEATURES
- 1-4 human players (hot-seat on one PC)
- Optional AI opponents
- Player-to-player trading + bank trading
- Multiple map modes
- No install, no account, works offline

Goal: reach 10 victory points.

Enjoy!
